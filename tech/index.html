Module 01

The Ecrion Case

Ecrion Software had a strong product and a positioning problem. The software was built for North American enterprise buyers. The brand wasn’t.

As Marketing Director, I rebuilt the full go-to-market: brand identity, messaging architecture, web presence, and demand gen. The challenge wasn’t technical — it was perceptual. Making an unfamiliar name feel credible, trustworthy, and native to the North American enterprise market in eight seconds.

The result: a complete repositioning. New identity, new website, new messaging framework, and a content engine built around the buyer’s actual problems — not the product’s feature list.
