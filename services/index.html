Document One — The Dailies Report

THE AUDIT DESK · SITE, BRAND, CAMPAIGN, PAPERWORK

You send what you’ve got — the site, the deck, the campaign, the brand file. The Audit Desk watches all of it the way a director watches dailies, and returns a signed report: circled takes (what’s working and why), named failures (what’s costing you and where), and a costed pick-up list (what to reshoot first, and what it takes). No scores, no dashboards, no 40-page PDF of charts. Verdicts.

The desk runs an AI-assisted review pipeline — built on the same systems as the Bench — but every verdict is mine and every page is signed. The entry point is free for DFW small businesses at sites.timshephard.co; the full Dailies Report is the working engagement.

THE BRIEF DESK · DIRECTION YOU CAN OPTION

Not a mood board — a territory you can option and build on, or walk away from having lost nothing but a conversation.
