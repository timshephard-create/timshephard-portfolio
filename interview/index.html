Q — What do you actually do?

Senior creative direction: brand systems, national campaigns, and the production discipline to ship them. Fifteen-plus years across five verticals — nonprofit, federal, tech/SaaS, entertainment/broadcast, and brand — most recently pointed at AI-native products of my own. The camera reports carry the record; the bench log carries what I’m building now.

Q — Are you available, and in what shape?

Yes — full-time roles and fractional creative direction are both open, alongside project-based work and advisory. Based in Dallas–Fort Worth, TX; built for remote. The fastest route is the crew call.

Q — The Keep America Beautiful numbers. What’s actually yours?

Fair question — most portfolios blur this, so here it is on the record. Directly mine to claim: the “Recycle Like Everyone’s Watching” PSA campaign I directed earned 160,000+ airings across all 50 states, $77.5M in earned media value, and 4.5M+ views on the branded Giphy channel. Not mine alone, and always labeled that way: the 11.9 million participants and $300M in documented economic impact are outcomes of the national movement my work supported — the volunteers, affiliates, and partners did that; my job was building the brand and creative infrastructure that let them show up.

Q — How does AI actually run in your work?

As crew, not as director. The Bench ventures run AI-assisted pipelines I designed and operate — feed scoring, research, production — and every output ships with an editor’s signature. Same on the services counter: the Audit Desk uses machine review to cover ground, and the verdicts are mine. I build with these tools daily, which is exactly why I don’t oversell what they can do yet.

Q — What’s with the production-floor thing?

The site is being rebuilt as one continuous production floor — a set you’ll navigate with editing verbs instead of menus. What you’re reading now is Edition Zero: the paperwork of that production, printed plain so it’s fast, crawlable, and readable anywhere. The paper does keep your hours, though — the palette follows your local clock through five lighting keys. Come back after dark.

Q — Do you have game industry credits?

No, and I won’t pretend otherwise. What I have is the transferable core — world-building, systems thinking, production discipline, an original children’s IP in development (Tim & The Time Machine) — and a live demonstration of interactive intent: this site’s rebuild. The ambition is real; the credits will be earned, not claimed.

Q — How does an engagement start?

Two documents and one form. If you want your current creative examined, book the Dailies Report. If you want a direction for something new, open a Creative Territory brief. If you’re hiring for a role, go straight to the crew call — it’s one page, and it goes directly to me.
