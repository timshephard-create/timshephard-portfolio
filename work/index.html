01 — The Monitor Wall · Keep America Beautiful

SENIOR CREATIVE DIRECTOR · 2020–2023 · NATIONAL — ALL 50 STATES

A 70-year-old national nonprofit, dormant to digital-native audiences, with 20,000+ affiliate organizations and no coherent identity between them. The engagement was a complete rebrand — identity system, digital infrastructure, PSA campaign, affiliate toolkit — repositioning a cleanup campaign as a national behavior-change movement.

The flagship: “Recycle Like Everyone’s Watching.” Broadcasters ran it 160,000+ times across all 50 states — not on media spend, but because the creative was built to be worth airing. The branded Giphy channel put the campaign into everyday conversation, 4.5 million views at a time.
